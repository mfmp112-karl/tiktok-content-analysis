Method, limits and credits

How I got these numbers: read via invented data (see examples/make_sample.py) — the same public metrics any visitor can see. Nothing downloaded, no engagement automated. Themes were grouped by tf-idf + k-means (pure python). I test every comparison with a standard statistics test (Welch’s t-test), on a log scale. Without that, one viral post can throw off a whole group. I never call a group proven with fewer than eight posts, no matter how big the gap looks. “Solid evidence” and “early signal” are two proof levels; anything weaker I call not settled. I converted upload times in this machine's local timezone, so “best hour” is in your clock, not UTC.

What I genuinely cannot see: Watch time, retention, traffic sources, follower growth, and who your audience is — TikTok shows these only to the account owner. I can’t get them for an account I don’t own. No tool can. A lot of creator advice leans on those numbers. Where it does, I tested what I could measure instead, and said plainly which is which.
